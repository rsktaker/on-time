# App Store submission checklist

Notes for shipping On Time to the App Store. This is a one-person checklist; expand as needed.

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Active **Apple Developer Program** membership ($99/year). Free Apple ID isn't enough for App Store distribution.
- [ ] Xcode 15 or newer signed in to your Apple ID under **Settings → Accounts**.
- [ ] App Store Connect access at https://appstoreconnect.apple.com.

## One-time setup

1. **Bundle ID** — register `com.rsktaker.ontime` at https://developer.apple.com/account/resources/identifiers/list. No special capabilities needed (no iCloud, no push, no in-app purchase).
2. **App Store Connect** — create a new app:
   - Platform: iOS
   - Name: `On Time`
   - Primary language: English (U.S.)
   - Bundle ID: `com.rsktaker.ontime`
   - SKU: `ontime-ios-1`
3. **Capabilities** — none. The only entitlement-adjacent item is the calendar usage description (already in `project.yml` → `NSCalendarsUsageDescription`).

## Per-release steps

1. Bump `MARKETING_VERSION` and `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` in `project.yml`. Re-run `xcodegen generate`.
2. In Xcode: **Product → Archive**. Once archived, **Distribute App → App Store Connect → Upload**.
3. In App Store Connect, fill in the new version's metadata (see below) and submit for review.

> Stop being late. On Time turns your calendar into a wall of alarms — each one rings a few minutes before the event. Pick the buffer, pick the calendars, done.

**Description:**
> On Time is the simplest possible way to stop being late.
>
> Every night, it wipes your alarms and creates fresh ones — one for each event on your calendar today, ringing a few minutes before each event starts. You pick the buffer. You pick which calendars count.
>
> No accounts. No cloud. No analytics. No nags. Two settings. Built by one person who got tired of being late.

> How it works:
> – Pair with the free On Time shortcut (one-tap install)
> – Set your buffer (1–30 minutes before each event)
> – Toggle off calendars you don't want alarms from
> – The shortcut runs automatically every night at 12:01 AM
> – Tap "Run Now" any time to refresh today's alarms
>
> Privacy: everything runs on your device. No data leaves your phone.
>

## Review notes (when submitting)

> On Time uses the iOS Shortcuts app to create alarms because Apple does not expose alarm creation to third-party apps. The user installs a free companion shortcut from an iCloud share link (no off-device service). The app passes the user's current settings to the shortcut via the standard `shortcuts://run-shortcut` URL scheme.
>
> A demo Apple ID is not required — the app works on any account with calendars set up.
